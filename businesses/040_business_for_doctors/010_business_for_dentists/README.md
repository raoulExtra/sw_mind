# Business for Dentists

## Context

This business domain operates within the **healthcare dentistry** context, focusing on practice management for dentists and dental professionals.

## Key Processes

- Patient dental appointment scheduling and management
- Dental record handling and compliance
- Dental billing and insurance processing
- Dental staff coordination and scheduling
- Dental equipment and facility management
- Dental procedure planning and tracking

- `biz_mind/v1/res/places/` - Dental clinic and office locations
