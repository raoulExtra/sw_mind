# Requirements: Biz Mind Mindmap

> Version: V00.04.00

## Overview

Requirements for mind mapping visualization of business types, roles, and AI-driven workflows with programming support.

## Business Mindmap Models

### Worker Accommodation
- Model rental relationships between landlords and tenants
- Visualize legal compliance requirements
- Map landlord-tenant interactions and obligations
- Track facilities and housing resources

### Service Businesses
- Visualize service delivery workflows
- Model customer interaction patterns
- Map service-specific processes and requirements

### Housing Resources
- Visualize facilities and housing inventory
- Map occupancy and maintenance schedules
- Track worker accommodation properties

### Financial/Tax Cycles
- Visualize periodic business processes
- Map tax obligations and deadlines
- Show financial reporting cycles
