# Convention: We do things straightforwardly

> Version: V00.01.00

## Purpose

Establish a principle of simplicity and straightforwardness in our work, favoring clear, uncomplicated solutions over unnecessary complexity.

## Rule

We choose the simplest solution that effectively solves the problem, avoiding over-engineering, premature optimization, and unnecessary abstractions.

## Guidelines

- **Prefer readability**: Write code and documentation that is easy to understand at a glance.
- **Avoid premature optimization**: First make it work, then make it right, then only if necessary make it fast.
- **Use standard tools**: Leverage built-in language features and standard libraries before introducing new dependencies.
- **Keep it small**: Functions, classes, and modules should have a single, well-defined responsibility.
- **Favor simplicity in design**: Choose straightforward architectures and patterns over complex ones when they meet the requirements.
- **Eliminate waste**: Remove dead code, commented-out code, and unused dependencies regularly.
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Prioritize clear, explicit solutions over clever or tricky implementations.

## Rationale

Straightforward work products are:
- Easier to understand and maintain
- Less prone to bugs and misunderstandings
- Faster to develop and review
- More accessible to new team members
- Simpler to debug and extend

By embracing simplicity, we reduce cognitive load and increase overall productivity and quality.
